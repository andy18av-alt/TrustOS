# Executive Summary

TrustOS is an **Enterprise AI-Native Trust & Safety Operating System** that orchestrates specialised AI agents to investigate policy violations, analyse evidence, evaluate risk, and generate explainable decisions while keeping humans in control of high-risk cases.

Designed for marketplaces, social platforms, fintech, e-commerce, and digital ecosystems, TrustOS reduces investigation time, improves decision consistency, strengthens governance, and creates a fully auditable decision trail.

---

# The Problem

Modern Trust & Safety teams rely on fragmented tools, manual investigations, static policy documents, and disconnected evidence sources.

As digital platforms scale, investigators face significant operational challenges:

- Reviewing thousands of cases consistently
- Correlating evidence across multiple systems
- Applying policies uniformly across investigations
- Producing transparent and explainable decisions
- Maintaining audit-ready governance
- Reducing investigation turnaround time

These challenges increase operational costs, introduce inconsistency, and make scalable governance difficult.

TrustOS addresses these challenges through **AI-native orchestration**, combining specialised AI agents, explainable decision-making, and human oversight into a single enterprise platform.
