# Executive Summary

TrustOS is an **Enterprise AI-Native Trust & Safety Operating System** that transforms manual investigations into autonomous, explainable, and auditable AI-powered decision workflows.

Built for marketplaces, social platforms, fintech, e-commerce, and other digital ecosystems, TrustOS orchestrates specialised AI agents to investigate policy violations, analyse evidence, assess risk, and generate explainable decisions while keeping humans in control of high-risk cases.

The platform reduces investigation turnaround time, improves policy consistency, strengthens governance, and creates a complete audit trail for enterprise-scale Trust & Safety operations.

---

# The Problem

Modern Trust & Safety teams rely on fragmented tools, manual investigations, static policy documentation, and disconnected evidence sources.

As digital platforms continue to scale, investigators face significant operational challenges:

- Reviewing thousands of cases consistently
- Correlating evidence across multiple internal systems
- Applying policies uniformly across investigations
- Producing transparent and explainable decisions
- Maintaining audit-ready governance
- Reducing investigation turnaround time without compromising quality

These operational inefficiencies increase investigation costs, reduce decision consistency, slow response times, and create governance risks for rapidly growing platforms.

TrustOS addresses these challenges through **AI-native orchestration**, combining specialised AI agents, explainable decision intelligence, and human oversight into a unified enterprise operating system.

---

# Meet TrustOS

TrustOS is an enterprise platform that coordinates specialised AI agents to automate complex Trust & Safety investigations from evidence collection through final decision recommendation.

Rather than replacing investigators, TrustOS augments them with AI agents specialised in evidence analysis, policy interpretation, visual inspection, risk assessment, and executive reporting. Every recommendation is explainable, traceable, and fully auditable, enabling organisations to scale Trust & Safety operations without sacrificing governance or accountability.

---

# 🏗️ Architecture

![TrustOS Architecture](assets/Arch%20Diagram.png)

TrustOS orchestrates multiple specialised AI agents through a structured investigation workflow. Each agent performs a focused responsibility before passing its findings to the Autonomous Risk Council, which generates an explainable recommendation for the Compliance Decision Engine. High-risk or ambiguous cases are escalated to human reviewers, ensuring governance while dramatically reducing manual effort.
